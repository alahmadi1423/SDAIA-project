# Library Services Guide (sample - replace with your own documents)

## Opening hours
The library is open Sunday to Thursday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and closed on Friday and Saturday.

## Borrowing
Students may borrow up to 5 books for 14 days. A loan can be renewed once if no one else has reserved the book.

## Late fines
The late fine is 2 SAR per day per book, capped at 50 SAR per book.

## Group study rooms
A study room can be booked for a maximum of 2 hours through the student portal. Each room seats 6 people.
